\\section*{Normalized Throughput Formula}

In the Shale benchmarking framework, the effective per-flow capacity under VLB spraying with spray depth $h$ is modeled as
\\[
    r_{\\text{eff}}(s, d) = \\frac{r_{\\text{raw}}(s, d)}{h + 1},
\\]
where $r_{\\text{raw}}$ is the waterfilling-allocated rate for the $(s, d)$ pair and $h$ is the spray depth. Each flow in Shale consumes $h+1$ hops, so the effective per-flow capacity is reduced by the same factor.

The resulting normalized throughput therefore scales as
\\[
    \\Theta \\approx \\frac{1}{h+1},
\\]
which serves as a lower bound on throughput regardless of traffic pattern.
